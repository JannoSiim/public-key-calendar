Estimates shift the last confirmed full-paper deadline by the series cycle. They are planning approximations. The baseline label refers to the **conference edition year**, which can differ from its deadline year. Cutoff times are not projected for estimates.

The earliest future submission opportunity is shown for each series. A conference whose deadline has passed is replaced by its next edition's estimate, even if the previous conference has yet to happen. Venues are not carried to future editions automatically.

Some older organizer pages time out on direct retrieval; their indexed official CFPs and IACR entries supply baseline evidence. ASIACRYPT 2027's edition page returned 404, so its link points to the last available edition, with a **Last edition** label.

This is a checked snapshot. Date rollover is not an online monitoring service; an updater must still check official sources and publish revised data.

# Hover details added 13 November 2026

Conference date tooltips use the existing sourced edition start/end dates below.
Their confirmation status is independent of the submission deadline status.
Projected future conference dates remain explicitly approximate.

`data/beer-prices.json` records 13 city estimates read on 13 November 2026 from
the linked Numbeo city pages, with `displayCurrency=EUR&displayUnit=1`.
The measure is **Domestic Draft Beer (0.5 Liter)** in the Restaurants section,
not bottled supermarket beer. Values are Numbeo's EUR conversions and are
crowdsourced estimates, not future conference-date quotes. Every tooltip links
its city source and shows the check date. Egham had no comparable Numbeo city
price; it displays unavailable. Unannounced cities and the online journal have
no invented price. This feature update is not a full conference-source audit.
